Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Night Tech Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202638854Z), operating as NightShift Electrician, handles personal data in connection with this website and our call-out service.

1. What we collect

We only collect personal data that you choose to give us directly — typically when you message us on WhatsApp or call us. This may include your name, phone number, property address or postal code, property type, and a description of the electrical fault. We use the optional Power Check tool's postal code field only to pass along to the electrician if you choose to fill it in; it is not required.

2. How we use it

We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry, dispatch a licensed electrician, carry out the job, and handle billing, cancellations, or refunds as described in our Terms of Use.

3. What we don't do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies on this website. This site does not run analytics scripts beyond what may be required for basic hosting.

4. Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with most web hosting, GitHub may automatically log standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) as part of serving the site. This is governed by GitHub's own privacy practices, not ours.

5. Data retention

We keep job and contact records for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, billing, and our own record-keeping obligations, and no longer than needed for those purposes.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp or calling +65 8159 0208.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version posted on this page is the one that applies at any given time.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to us on WhatsApp or by phone at +65 8159 0208.
